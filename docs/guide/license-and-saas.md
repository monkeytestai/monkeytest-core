## Plain-language summary

- **You may** read, run, modify, and distribute this software under AGPL-3.0.
- **If you modify** the engine and **offer it as a network service** to users, AGPL generally requires that those users can **receive the corresponding source** of your modified version. That is the “SaaS loophole” fix compared to vanilla GPL.
- **Using** an unmodified copy internally or for experiments is broadly in line with free-software goals, but obligations depend on how you deploy and distribute — again, ask a lawyer for your scenario.

## MonkeyTest Cloud (SaaS)

**[MonkeyTest Cloud](https://monkeytest.ai)** is our **hosted product**: accounts, billing, reports, and operations. It is the path we recommend for:

- Non-developers and solo founders who want **outcomes**, not repos  
- Teams that need **SLAs**, support, and **no infrastructure**  

Using MonkeyTest Cloud is **not** a substitute for reading AGPL if you also ship **your own** modified version of the engine as a service.

## Why AGPL for the engine?

We want **maximum transparency** for developers and **sustainable investment** in the product. AGPL discourages opaque forks that compete without contributing improvements back to the ecosystem.
